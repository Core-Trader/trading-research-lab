# Product Vision

Trading Research Lab is a local-first quantitative research environment for
turning broker/backtest evidence into reproducible trading analysis. It is
research software, not investment advice, trade execution software, or a broker
record of truth.

## Personal-first, product-ready

The initial build is a fully enabled personal research tool. Its files,
canonical model, calculations, and research documents must remain usable if a
future distribution adds Free/Pro capability tiers. Entitlements are an
application boundary, not a calculation, storage, or document mutation rule.

The development provider returns every feature as enabled and every limit as
unlimited. No payment, account, licence server, activation, trial, analytics,
or telemetry is in scope.

## V1 experience

Inside Obsidian Desktop, a researcher can:

1. import immutable MT5 evidence;
2. inspect provenance, validation, and data-quality limitations;
3. create canonical datasets and reproducible analyses;
4. view worker-generated statistics, series, risk findings, and charts;
5. link analyses to Markdown experiments, conclusions, and reports in a vault;
6. distinguish broker-native evidence from optional prop-firm scenario overlays.

## Core domains

Dashboard, datasets, strategies, experiments, trade analysis, portfolio
analysis, Monte Carlo, What-If analysis, money management, risk/prop firms,
reports, and settings are enduring domain boundaries. Their order and UI shape
are not a commitment to immediate implementation.

## Non-negotiable user promises

- Research remains local by default.
- Raw source remains immutable and traceable.
- Quantitative answers come from one Python calculation authority.
- Results identify their data, assumptions, versions, and limitations.
- User-owned Markdown text is never overwritten by generated analysis.
- The personal vault remains separate from source code and release packages.
