# ADR-011 — Local-First and Zero Telemetry

**Status:** Accepted | **Date:** 2026-09-20

## Context

Trading datasets and research are sensitive and must remain usable offline.

## Decision

V1 has no telemetry, mandatory account, cloud dependency, analytics collection,
or independent updater.

## Alternatives considered

Cloud-first storage; usage analytics; custom update service.

## Consequences

Network features require separate future consent and architecture decisions.
