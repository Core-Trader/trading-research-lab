# ADR-009 — Product-Ready, Personal-First Entitlements

**Status:** Accepted | **Date:** 2026-09-20

## Context

Future Free/Pro tiers must not corrupt personal research or calculation logic.

## Decision

Provide an application-level capability/limit abstraction; development enables
everything. Do not implement commercial infrastructure.

## Alternatives considered

Hard-coded limits in calculations; payment/licensing work now.

## Consequences

Core data and research documents remain independent of commercial policy.
