## Phase C1: Core facts (commit 9b62d2f, D4)

- **Monte Carlo** (`m6-monte-carlo-order-permutation-4`):
  - the historical order's maximum drawdown, its rank (the share of paths
    strictly shallower), and DEEPER/SHALLOWER/EQUAL vs the median
  - nearest-rank p05/p50/p95 fan bands over **all** paths per sampled point,
    held as exact scaled integers in `array('q')`, about 20 MB at 10k paths
  - the widest band and where it occurs
  - p50 and p95 as a percentage of the opening balance (None without an
    opening-balance row)
- **Shared Pareto** (`shared-pareto-2`): `frontier_steps` for one MAX and
  one MIN objective (gain, cost, ratio, a diminishing flag), passed through
  `portfolio.explore`.
- **Equity metrics** (`mvp-equity-metrics-2`): the balance maximum
  drawdown, the equity-to-balance ratio, and `equity_deeper_than_balance`.
  The plugin no longer computes the ratio or applies its own 1.5× threshold,
  which had broken the hard rule.
- Tests check the new fields against the stored per-path parquet and
  hand-worked cases. Core: 221 tests.
